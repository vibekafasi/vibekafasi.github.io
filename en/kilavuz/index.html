KAFA ASİSTAN:
how to use it

KAFA is your free personal assistant that you load into Claude. Set it up once, then just write to it every day like you would to a friend. This guide walks you through everything, from setup to the panel. No tech skills needed.

Heads up: KAFA and our Telegram bots were made in Turkish. The screenshots show the Turkish claude.ai interface, so your button names may differ slightly, but they are in the same places.

🧠
KAFA in ClaudeThe brain. Knows you, plans your day, remembers what you said.
🖥️
KAFA panelThe screen. A Jarvis-style animated panel you control with your hand, face and voice.
📒
NotebookYour data. It stays in your account; we see nothing.

SECTION 1

Setup: 5 steps, 5 minutes

We recommend doing this on a computer (the settings menu is harder to find on a phone). A free Claude account is enough.

1

Get the KAFA file

Comment KAFA under our Instagram video or send KAFA to our Telegram bot (the bot is in Turkish). Once you join our channel, you get the kafa_asistan.zip file. Don't unzip it, save it as is.

💡 Don't unzip it. Claude takes it as a zip. If your computer unzipped it automatically, right-click the kafa-asistan folder inside and compress it again.
2

Turn on the "Run code in the cloud" switch

For KAFA to work, one switch in Claude needs to be on.

claude.ai→your name, bottom left→Settings→Capabilities

Scroll down a bit. Under the "Code execution and file creation" heading, the "Run code in the cloud" switch should be blue. Below it, it says it's required for Skills.

3

On the Skills page, "Upload skill"

Settings→Skills→+ Add→Upload skill
4

Drop the zip, hit "Upload"

Drag the file into the gray box (or click the box and pick it). Then hit Upload. Claude runs a quick safety scan.

5

Check that it's on

The kafa-asistan page opens. The switch in the top right should be blue. That's it, setup is done! If you like, start right away with Try in chat next to it.

SECTION 2

First chat: "Kafa kur"

Open a new chat and type Kafa kur (Turkish for "KAFA, set up"). KAFA asks 3 short rounds of questions to get to know you (2–3 minutes). If you don't know an answer, just type skip.

Kafa kur
KAFAHi! I'm KAFA, your personal assistant. I'll ask a few questions to get to know you.
1. What's your name? 2. What do you do? 3. What 1–3 things are you most busy with right now?
Ayse. I sell homemade cookies. My Instagram page and new product packs.
KAFA… (your goal, your day, how you want me to talk) …
Here's what I learned about you: … I'm saving your info to your Claude memory. 
KAFA CARD
I'm Ayse. I sell homemade cookies. My goal: 100 orders a month.
Talk to me warmly and briefly. Be honest.
⭐ Paste the KAFA CARD once into Settings → Account → the "Instructions for Claude" box. That way it recognizes you in every new chat and in voice mode.
SECTION 3

What do I type every day?

No commands to memorize, just write like you normally talk. The ones below are only examples. If you forget, type What can you do?

☀️ Day

Morning plan and evening wrap-up.

  • Good morning → top 3 tasks for the day, what's pending
  • Close the day → done, left, first thing tomorrow

✅ To-dos

Understands dates on its own ("Friday", "tomorrow").

  • add: call the shipping company, Friday
  • done: shipping · what's left?

📝 Notes and memory

Never forgets what you said.

  • note: flour 25 kg 900 lira
  • how much was the flour again?

💰 Money log

Adds up the totals for you.

  • bought flour for 300 lira · Zeynep paid 1,200
  • What did I spend this month? · Ahmet owes 2,000

👥 Customers

Tracks who to get back to and when.

  • Selin asked for a sample, I'll call tomorrow
  • Who do I need to get back to?

📸 Log from a photo

Send a receipt, invoice or screenshot.

  • Shows what it read and never saves without asking

🧠 Summaries and brain dumps

Sorts through long text.

  • Paste meeting notes + summarize
  • Brain dump: …

🎯 Goals and plans

Breaks a big goal into small steps.

  • Break my goal into steps
  • This week's posting plan

🔁 Habits and decisions

Counts your streak, remembers your decisions.

  • Track a 20-minute walk every day · walked
  • I made this decision: …

✍️ Writing and advice

Writes like you, talks straight.

  • Write a reply to Zeynep (it won't send it, you do)
  • What do you think of this idea? · How was my week?

Example: a receipt photo

📷 (receipt photo) Save this receipt
KAFAHere's what I read: Wholesale Store · 08.11 · Flour 950, Sugar 210, Boxes 180 = 1,340 lira. Log it as an expense?
yes
KAFA✔ Expense: 1,340 lira · supplies + packaging.
🔔 An honest note about reminders: KAFA can't send notifications to your phone on its own. It puts the task on your good-morning list and tells you to set a phone reminder too, so you don't miss it.
SECTION 4

The panel: your personal screen

The panel is KAFA's animated, Jarvis-style screen. Type Kafa, open my panel to Claude. You get a personal link; when you open it, the panel shows your tasks, goal, projects and notes. Works on both phone and computer.

Panel buttons

BUTTONWHAT IT DOES
🎙️TalkPress and say: "add: …", "note: …", "good morning", "done".
➕Add task / NoteOpens the text box with "add:" or "note:" already filled in.
📅My weekReads your day out loud.
💬Write to KAFAQuestions and chats open in Claude, with your KAFA.
📋Send to ClaudeCopies what you added and finished in the panel. Paste it into Claude and KAFA logs it in your notebook.
🔒PrivacyYour data lives inside the link and never goes to a server. Don't share your link with anyone.
SECTION 5

Hand gestures and facial expressions

In the panel, tap 📷 Turn on camera and allow access. The first time, a short tutorial pops up; then look straight ahead for 2 seconds so KAFA learns your face. The video never leaves your computer; everything runs in your browser.

Hand

🤏Pinch thumb + index fingerClicks. Over a card, it grabs and moves it; flick it fast and it closes.

👉Swipe your hand fast left or rightNext / previous card.

SECTION 6

Using your voice

There are two ways:

📱
Voice mode in the Claude appIn the Claude app on your phone, tap the voice icon and say: "Kafa, good morning". KAFA answers in short sentences, like a real conversation.
🖥️
The panel's microphoneTap 🎙️ Talk. The panel listens in English and answers out loud. Works best in Chrome and Edge.
🎙️ Your browser asks for mic permission. When the panel is opened from a web link, you allow it once and it remembers.
SECTION 7

Where is my data stored?

KAFA picks the best notebook for wherever it's running and tells you in one sentence at the end of setup.

💬
Claude memoryIn a claude.ai chat: your info is saved to your Claude memory under the heading "KAFA". You can view and delete it in Settings → Memory.
📁
A folder on your computerIf you work in a folder with the Claude desktop app or Claude Code, everything is stored as files in that folder.
🗒️
Notebook summaryIf there's no way to save, KAFA tells you plainly. Say save and it gives you a summary to paste into a new chat.

KAFA's unbreakable rules

  • Nothing without your OK: without your "yes", it never sends, deletes or buys anything.
  • Passwords, card and ID numbers: it never asks for them and won't save them even if you type them.
  • No making things up: it won't invent numbers, dates or people it doesn't know.
  • No sucking up: it tells you kindly but clearly when an idea is weak.

If you say Reset KAFA, it shows what will be deleted and asks you to confirm.

SECTION 8

What if it doesn't work?

I can't find "Upload skill"
In the Settings window, click Skills in the left menu, then + Add → Upload skill in the top right. If you don't see it, first turn on the Run code in the cloud switch from Section 1, Step 2.
Upload gives an error
The file must be a zip. If you unzipped it, right-click the kafa-asistan folder inside, choose "Compress" and upload the new zip. Renaming the file is fine.
I typed "Kafa kur" but it answered like regular Claude
On the Skills page, check that the kafa-asistan switch is blue. Then open a new chat and type again: Kafa kur.
It doesn't recognize me in a new chat
Paste the KAFA CARD you got at the end of setup into Settings → Account → the "Instructions for Claude" box. Starting your message with Kafa, also helps.
The panel link doesn't show up
For the panel link, Run code in the cloud must be on (Section 1, Step 2). If it is, type again: Kafa, open my panel.
The camera won't turn on
When your browser asks for camera access, click Allow. If you blocked it before, turn the camera on from the lock icon in the address bar. If another app (like Zoom) is using the camera, close it. The camera doesn't work inside Claude's chat window; open the panel from the link.
The mic keeps asking for permission
If you opened the panel from a file on your computer, the browser won't remember permission. Open the panel from the web link; it only asks once.
Hand or face gestures barely work
Make sure your face and hand are well lit and sit 50–80 cm (about 2–3 ft) from the camera. Tap Repeat tutorial in the camera box. For expressions, look straight ahead for 2 seconds so KAFA re-learns your face.
